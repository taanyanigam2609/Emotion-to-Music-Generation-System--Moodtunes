The Workflow

From visual capture to audio synthesis, discover how the MoodTunes pipeline operates in milliseconds.

1. Data Capture

The frontend captures your visual or textual input. Images are compressed instantly via HTML5 Canvas to ensure lightning-fast, lightweight payloads before securely transmitting to the Flask backend.

2. Psychological Mapping

OpenCV and DeepFace analyze the data, mapping facial micro-expressions into Russell’s Circumplex Model (Valence/Arousal) to determine your precise emotional quadrant.

3. Neural Composition

A Conditional LSTM network receives your emotion ID and stylistic parameters. It predicts the optimal sequence of musical notes, applying temperature scaling for unique creativity.

4. Browser Synthesis

The raw MIDI data is sent back to your browser where Tone.js natively synthesizes the audio. The track is routed through a Master Limiter and Reverb channel for studio-quality sound.
